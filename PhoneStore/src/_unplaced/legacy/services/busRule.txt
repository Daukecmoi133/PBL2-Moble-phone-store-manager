==========================================================

Pham vi: cac file trong source/services tai thoi diem ra soat.
Danh dau:
- ON: co the giu, hanh vi chinh hop ly.
- SUA: ham co nhung can sua dieu kien, ket qua hoac cach dong bo.
- THIEU: chua co ham nghiep vu can thiet.
- TUY CHON: chi them neu pham vi bai tap yeu cau.

Day la danh gia theo source hien tai, khong phai danh sach tinh nang da hoan thanh.


1. PHONESERVICE.H
-----------------
Hien co: constructor, rebuildIndices, getAllPhones, getPhoneById,
filterByPriceRange, searchByName, searchByBrand, addPhone, updatePhone,
deletePhone, hasStock, updateStock.

Constructor / rebuildIndices() - ON, can giu.
- Khoi tao HashTable theo ID va BST theo gia tu PhoneRepository.
- Tao service sau khi repository da load du lieu.
- Sau moi thay doi san pham/gia, index phai dong bo lai.
- Index HashTable dang giu ban sao Phone; khong duoc xem ban sao nay la nguon du lieu chinh.

getAllPhones() - ON.
- Chi doc danh sach tu repository.
- Khong can them ham getAllPhones khac neu UI chi can doc danh sach.

getPhoneById(id, errorMessage) - SUA.
- Kiem tra ID co dang chu so bang checkIDnum roi tra ket qua tu idTable.
- Check ID chu so la rang buoc dang co cua ham, can xac nhan ID san pham co bat buoc chi gom chu so khong.
- Neu ID hop le nhung khong tim thay, hien ham tra nullptr ma khong dien loi "khong tim thay".
- Can thong nhat errorMessage cho ca ID sai dinh dang va ID khong ton tai.
- Neu service can tra error, khong nen chi tra nullptr voi errorMessage cu tu lan goi truoc.

filterByPriceRange(minPrice, maxPrice, errorMessage) - SUA nhe.
- Neu bien gia am, ham hien tai doi bien am thanh 0 va dien thong bao; tiep tuc tim kiem.
- Tu choi khi minPrice > maxPrice.
- Can thong nhat hanh vi: gia am la input sai thi tu choi, hay chap nhan va chuan hoa ve 0.
- Can xoa/reset errorMessage khi thao tac hop le; hien tai thanh cong/khong co ket qua khong ro qua errorMessage.
- Can kiem tra gia tri NaN/vo cuc neu dau vao co the den tu tinh toan.

searchByName(keyword) - SUA neu can normalization ten.
- Tim chuoi con khong phan biet hoa thuong tren ten dang luu.
- Chua bo dau cach va chuan hoa ca ten san pham lan keyword; "iPhone 18" va "iphone18" khong nhat thiet match.
- Neu yeu cau tim khong phu thuoc khoang trang, can dung cung mot ham normalize cho luc nap/luu/tim; giu ten hien thi rieng, khong lam mat ten goc.
- Keyword rong hien co the match tat ca ten; can quy dinh ro co cho phep khong.

searchByBrand(brand) - ON, can quy dinh input rong.
- Tim chuoi con khong phan biet hoa thuong.
- Quy dinh keyword rong tra tat ca hay khong tra ket qua.

addPhone(phone, errorMessage) - SUA.
- Hien dang tu choi ID trung, ten rong, gia ban <= 0, quantity < 0; sau khi repo luu thanh cong thi them vao index.
- THIEU: ID rong/dinh dang; gia nhap am; validate du lieu khac theo yeu cau.
- Can tra loi ro neu them thanh cong va clear errorMessage cu.
- Kiem tra ID trung phai dung cung chuan ID voi du lieu da nap.
- Neu repo.addPhone() that bai, repo co the da them phone vao RAM truoc khi save file that bai; khi do index va repository lech nhau. Can sua repository de giao dich add/save nguyen tu hoac rollback.

addPhone(phone) - ON ve tien ich, nhung nguy co mat loi.
- Goi overload co errorMessage roi chi tra bool.
- Chi giu neu caller khong can hien thi ly do; UI nghiep vu nen dung overload co errorMessage.

updatePhone(phone, errorMessage) - SUA.
- Hien tai kiem tra sellingPrice > 0, quantity >= 0; repository update thanh cong thi rebuild index.
- THIEU: kiem tra ID ton tai truoc; ten khong rong; importPrice khong am; ID co hop le va co duoc phep doi hay khong.
- Neu ID khong ton tai, can loi "khong tim thay", khong dung chung loi ghi file.
- Repository co the cap nhat vector trong RAM roi moi save file; save that bai co the de index cu va du lieu repo moi lech nhau. Can rollback/atomic save.

updatePhone(phone) - ON ve tien ich, nhung nguy co mat loi.
- Goi overload co errorMessage va bo thong tin loi.
- UI nghiep vu nen dung overload co errorMessage.

deletePhone(id, errorMessage) - ON ve rule tham chieu, SUA ve input/loi luu.
- Tu choi neu khong tim thay trong idTable hoac ReferenceChecker bao phone da duoc tham chieu boi Order/Invoice/StockReceipt.
- Xoa trong repo roi rebuild index neu thanh cong.
- THIEU: validate ID/dinh dang va thong diep rieng cho ID sai.
- Neu save file that bai, repository co the da xoa khoi RAM; service tra false nhung index chua rebuild. Can rollback/atomic save.
- Can xac nhan ReferenceChecker duoc khoi tao voi dung cac repository va co tinh ca lich su da huy.

deletePhone(id) - ON ve tien ich, nhung nguy co mat loi.
- Goi overload co errorMessage va bo thong tin loi.

hasStock(id, requestedQuantity, errorMessage) - SUA.
- Tu choi requestedQuantity <= 0 hoac > INT_MAX; tra false neu khong tim thay; neu tim thay thi so sanh ton kho.
- LOI: neu ton kho khong du, errorMessage van ghi "Thanh cong".
- Can phan biet khong du ton kho voi input sai va san pham khong ton tai.
- Loai bo/giu gioi han INT_MAX phai thong nhat voi kieu quantity cua Phone.

updateStock(id, quantityDelta, errorMessage) - SUA, uu tien cao.
- Tu choi delta vuot mien int; tim san pham; khong cho ton kho moi am; goi repository; rebuild index sau khi luu thanh cong.
- Can kiem tra ton kho moi khong vuot INT_MAX. Hien tai phep cong tao ket qua long long nhung gan vao int co the tran.
- Neu repo.updatePhone() that bai, object Phone trong HashTable copy da bi setQuantity truoc khi repo duoc cap nhat; can dam bao index va repository khong bi lech.
- Repository co the da sua RAM truoc khi save file that bai; can rollback/atomic save.
- Can quy uoc delta = 0: tu choi hay chap nhan.
- Can dien errorMessage ro rang cho thanh cong/that bai.

updateStock(id, quantityDelta) - ON ve tien ich, nhung nguy co mat loi.
- Goi overload co errorMessage va bo thong tin loi; chi dung khi caller chap nhan khong biet nguyen nhan that bai.

Ham can them cho PhoneService:
- validatePhone(phone): dung chung mot bo rule cho add/update.
- getPhoneById(id) overload khong error chi neu can cho caller don gian; khong tao overload trung lap neu khong co use case.
- Chua can them getPhoneNameFormat neu normalization chi dung cho search; ten hien thi phai giu ten goc va co getter rieng chi khi model thuc su luu them normalized name.

getCustomerByUsername, getCustomerByPhone, getCustomerById,

- Du lieu trung trong file duoc xu ly am tham; can bao loi/log hoac quy dinh giu ban ghi nao.
- Index luu ban sao Customer, khong phai Customer trong repository.

getAllCustomers() - ON.

- Can tu choi username/password rong som va thong nhat message/error result neu UI can phan biet.
- Password khong duoc dua vao log/thong diep.
- Dang lap lai mot phan trach nhiem voi EmployeeService::authenticate; neu co AuthService thi AuthService nen dieu phoi, khong copy credential logic.

getCustomerByUsername(username) - ON, can chuan hoa input neu username khong phan biet hoa thuong.

getCustomerByPhone(phone) - SUA nhe.

getCustomerById(id) - ON.

registerCustomer(customer, errorMessage) - SUA.

- THIEU: customer ID rong/trung; password rong/chinh sach password; validate name/phone/email; points >= 0.
- Can chuan hoa username/phone truoc khi so trung neu muon nhan cac cach viet tuong duong.
- Neu repo.addCustomer() that bai sau khi da them RAM, service khong cap nhat index nhung repository co the da thay doi; can atomic save/rollback.

registerCustomer(customer) - ON ve tien ich, nhung mat thong tin loi.
- Goi overload co errorMessage va bo thong diep.
- UI dang ky nen dung overload co errorMessage.

updateCustomer(updatedCustomer, errorMessage) - SUA, uu tien cao.
- Hien tai chi goi repo.updateCustomer(); neu thanh cong thi rebuild index.
- THIEU: kiem tra customer ton tai; username/phone moi khong duoc trung voi nguoi khac; validate cac truong va khong cho doi ID neu ID la khoa.
- Rebuild chi khi save thanh cong; neu repository sua RAM truoc khi save that bai thi index cu va vector moi lech nhau.
- Customer lookup username/phone tra ban sao; can xem lai kha nang client sua object sai noi tru.

updateCustomer(updatedCustomer) - ON ve tien ich, nhung mat thong tin loi.
- Goi overload co errorMessage va bo thong diep.

Ham can them cho CustomerService:
- deleteCustomer(id, errorMessage) va overload don gian; repository da co delete.

3. EMPLOYEESERVICE.H
--------------------
Hien co: constructor, rebuildIndices, getAllEmployees, authenticate,
getEmployeeByUsername, getEmployeeById, addEmployee (2 overload),
deleteEmployee (2 overload).

Constructor / rebuildIndices() - ON, can giu.
- Tao index username va ID tu cac Employee* khong null.

- Phai rebuild sau thay doi username/ID va sau xoa; khong dung pointer sau khi repo da delete object.
- Can bo qua key rong/phat hien duplicate thay vi de index co key rong hoac overwrite.

getAllEmployees() - SUA API.
- Tra const Vector<Employee*>&, nhung const vector khong lam object Employee tro toi thanh const; caller van co the sua employee qua pointer.
- Neu ham chi doc, tra const Employee* hoac DTO/read-only view; khong expose mutable entity tu service.

authenticate(username, password) - ON co ban, SUA nhe.
- Kiem tra index, Employee* va Account::authenticate; inactive/sai thong tin thi nullptr.
- Can tu choi input rong va khong tiet lo chi tiet credential.
- Trung lap trach nhiem voi CustomerService::authenticate; AuthService co the dieu phoi chung.

getEmployeeByUsername(username) - ON, can gioi han mutable access.
- Tra pointer Employee* trong repository.
- Caller sua object qua pointer se khong tu dong validate/save; phai thong qua updateEmployee.

getEmployeeById(id) - ON, can gioi han mutable access.
- Nhu tren; pointer mat hieu luc sau khi repository xoa object.

addEmployee(employee, errorMessage) - SUA, uu tien cao.
- Hien tai tu choi nullptr, username rong/trung, ID trung; neu repo add thanh cong thi cap nhat index.
- THIEU: ID rong; validate role hop le, salary >= 0, phone/email/name; username normalize; trung phone/email neu policy yeu cau.
- Ownership raw pointer chua ro tai call site; can quy dinh repo co nhan quyen so huu pointer hay khong. Khong delete emp neu service bao that bai neu caller van dang so huu.
- Neu repo da push pointer nhung save file that bai, service khong cap nhat index nhung repo co the da so huu object; can atomic save/rollback va ownership ro.

addEmployee(employee) - ON ve tien ich, nhung mat thong tin loi.
- Goi overload co errorMessage va bo thong diep.

deleteEmployee(id, errorMessage) - SUA.
- Kiem tra ID co trong index, repo xoa, sau do rebuild index.
- Can kiem tra ket qua save/rollback: EmployeeRepository xoa va delete object truoc khi save; neu save that bai service tra false nhung repo/index co the khong con dong bo.
- THIEU: quy tac khong xoa admin cuoi cung/tai khoan dang dung neu nghiep vu yeu cau; kiem tra tham chieu lich su neu can giu audit.

deleteEmployee(id) - ON ve tien ich, nhung mat thong tin loi.
- Goi overload co errorMessage va bo thong diep.

Ham can them cho EmployeeService:
- updateEmployee(employee, errorMessage) va overload don gian; validate unique username va cac truong, persist, rebuild index.
- activate/deactivate/reset password chi khi co yeu cau.
- Phan quyen cho thao tac them/sua/xoa nen duoc enforcement o authorization boundary; hien cac ham khong nhan actor/permission.


4. ORDERSERVICE.H
-----------------
Hien co: loadPendingQueue, constructor, setPhoneService, getPendingQueue,
getAllOrders, getOrderById, getOrdersByCustomer, createOrder (2 overload),
processNextPendingOrder (2 overload), cancelOrder, calculateTotalRevenue.

loadPendingQueue() / constructor - ON co ban, SUA de chong queue stale.
- Nap cac don PENDING theo thu tu repository.
- Don co the bi huy sau khi nap ma van con trong queue; khi dequeue phai doc lai status va bo qua don khong con PENDING.
- Tranh nap queue hai lan neu co reload/reinitialize.

setPhoneService(phoneService) - THUA/NGUY CO neu constructor luon truyen dependency.
- Pointer nullable cho phep tao OrderService khong co kiem tra/tru kho, nhung service van co the confirm don.
- Neu quy trinh don hang bat buoc inventory, nhan PhoneService/InventoryService reference bat buoc trong constructor va bo setPhoneService.
- Chi giu nullable neu co use case ro rang cho don khong co hang hoa.

getPendingQueue() - NEN BO HOAC SUA API.
- Tra Queue<Order>& mutable, ben ngoai co the push/pop/sua status, pha FIFO va lam queue khac repository.
- Thay bang pending count/read-only list hoac chi expose process/cancel methods.

getAllOrders() - ON.
- Chi doc danh sach tu repository.

getOrderById(orderId) - ON co ban.
- Tra con tro mutable den repository; caller co the doi status/detail ma khong qua business rule/save.
- Nen tra const view cho doc; moi thay doi di qua OrderService.

getOrdersByCustomer(customerId) - ON co ban.
- Loc tuyen tinh theo customerId, O(n).
- Can validate customerId/kiem tra customer ton tai neu service co dependency.

createOrder(order, errorMessage) - SUA, uu tien rat cao.
- Hien tai kiem tra ID rong, details khong rong; neu co PhoneService thi check stock; luu repo va dua PENDING vao queue.
- THIEU: ID trung; customer ton tai; phone ID ton tai; quantity > 0; unitPrice hop le; discount khong am/khong vuot subtotal; status ban dau hop le; detail trung lap.
- Neu phoneService == nullptr, don hien duoc tao khong kiem tra ton kho.
- Kiem tra ton kho luc tao khong reserve stock; nhieu don PENDING co the cung dat cung mot so hang. Phai chon ro: reserve khi tao hoac check lai khi duyet.
- Chi push queue khi luu don thanh cong la dung; nhung repository co the thay doi RAM truoc khi save file that bai, can rollback.

createOrder(order) - ON ve tien ich, nhung mat thong tin loi.
- Goi overload co errorMessage va bo thong diep.

processNextPendingOrder(order, errorMessage) - SUA NGHIEM TRONG, UU TIEN CAO NHAT.
- Muc tieu la xu ly don dau FIFO con PENDING, kiem tra va tru kho, chuyen CONFIRMED, persist.
- Hien pop queue truoc khi tim don; khong kiem tra lai status/stock; bo qua ket qua updateStock va orderRepo.updateOrder; van co the tra true khi cac thao tac nay that bai.
- Khong co rollback neu tru kho mot phan cho don nhieu san pham.
- Neu phoneService nullptr, don van duoc confirm ma khong tru kho.
- Can chi confirm sau khi tat ca stock updates va save order thanh cong; loi phai khong mat don khoi queue hoac phai co retry/requeue rule.
- Phai co giao dich/rollback cho nhieu detail de kho va don khong lech.

processNextPendingOrder(order) - ON ve tien ich, nhung mat thong tin loi.
- Goi overload co errorMessage va bo thong diep.

cancelOrder(orderId, errorMessage) - SUA NGHIEM TRONG.
- Hien tai chi tu choi khong tim thay/da CANCELLED; moi trang thai khac deu co the huy.
- Hoan kho neu CONFIRMED va phoneService co; nhung bo qua ket qua updateStock va orderRepo.updateOrder.
- Don PENDING da huy co the van o queue; khi xu ly queue phai bo qua status khong con PENDING.
- Can quy dinh trang thai duoc phep huy; khong huy COMPLETED; hoan kho dung mot lan va chi persist CANCELLED khi hoan kho thanh cong.
- Can co rollback neu hoan nhieu mat hang va mot cap nhat that bai.

calculateTotalRevenue() - SUA theo policy, hoac CHUYEN sang ReportService.
- Hien cong don CONFIRMED va COMPLETED.
- Chua loc theo ngay/phuong thuc thanh toan; CONFIRMED co the chua thanh toan.
- Chot dinh nghia doanh thu (paid/completed hay confirmed) va loai CANCELLED/refund.
- Neu co ReportService, chuyen logic tong hop bao cao vao do, tranh hai noi tinh doanh thu.

Ham can them cho OrderService:
- updateOrderStatus(orderId, nextStatus, errorMessage) hoac cac ham hanh dong cu the: confirm, prepare, ready, ship, complete.
- Kiem tra state transition hop le; khong cho set status tuy y tu UI.
- find/list/filter theo status/date neu UI nghiep vu can.
- Kiem tra ID trung, customer va detail.
- Khong can ham CRUD chung neu cac hanh dong domain cu the da bao phu du.
